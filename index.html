Selected work

Mostly from two internships, in 2025 and 2026.

Miller Electric, 2026

Motor control for a new wire feeder

The new OptX feeder board swapped slow relay-based direction control for a full H-bridge driver. I wrote the FreeRTOS application around it, with PI control that made feeding faster, more precise, and more responsive.

C, FreeRTOS, PI control, PWM

A toy motor-speed model, not the real feeder. Drag the gains and watch the step response change.

Miller Electric, 2026

A new power board and its serial protocol

A redesigned Auxiliary Power Unit replaced an AC-output cooler supply with a smaller, lighter DC-output board on a modern processor. I wrote its C firmware, including RS-485 communication over proprietary Data Link and Frame layers, to support the Dynasty and Maxstar TIG welder line.

C, RS-485, custom framing

Miller Electric, 2026

Firmware updates over Ethernet

I configured a silicon Ethernet switch and built a TCP/IP command interface on LwIP. It runs in two places, under FreeRTOS in the application and bare-metal in the bootloader, with the same interface in both so boards can be updated in the field.

C, LwIP, FreeRTOS, bootloader

Pierce Manufacturing, 2025

A CAN diagnostics tool for technicians in the field

Truck electrical faults are hard to chase without the right tools. I built a dual-channel CAN diagnostics tool on a Raspberry Pi that checks the bus and records traffic, and I designed all of its hardware too.

C++, GTK-3, KiCad, Raspberry Pi

Measures
Bus voltage and resistance, to find missing or excess termination
Records
Bus traffic as .trc trace files that engineers open in PCAN Explorer
Hardware
Custom PCB designed in KiCad, battery power for untethered use, 3D-printed enclosure
Interface
A C++/GTK-3 interface for technicians

Pierce Manufacturing, 2025

Redesigning a hardware-in-the-loop simulator board

Pierce's simulator lets engineers test trucks without physical units in the shop. I redesigned and debugged its first-revision PCB, and wrote up every flaw and fix so the next engineer starts further ahead.

KiCad, board debug, documentation

Miller Electric, 2026

Tracking down a rare boot failure

A small number of UI boards would not boot into their application. I investigated it and wrote an ECO that fixed the bug and let the faulty boards go back to the manufacturing line.

Debugging, ECO process
